job-autofill

job-autofill is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own Mac. It helps with job applications: it searches job boards, fills application forms in the owner's browser, and keeps track of each application's status.

For the status tracking it can read the owner's Gmail with read-only access, to find replies from employers (application received, rejected, online assessment, interview). It is used by its owner only and is not offered to anyone else.
